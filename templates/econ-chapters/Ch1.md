# Ch1 The basic economic problem

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 1 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 1。

## 大纲目标
- 理解稀缺（scarcity）是经济学的核心问题：欲望无限、资源有限 → 必须选择 → 产生机会成本
- 掌握生产要素及其报酬
- 用生产可能性曲线（PPC）说明稀缺、选择、机会成本与增长
- 理解专业化与分工的收益与代价

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| scarcity | The basic economic problem: unlimited wants but limited resources | 稀缺（核心问题） |
| opportunity cost | The next best alternative given up when a choice is made | 机会成本 |
| wants | Desires for goods and services, effectively unlimited | 欲望 |
| needs | Items essential for survival, such as food and shelter | 需要 |
| resources | The inputs used to produce goods and services; limited in supply | 资源 |
| factors of production | Land, labour, capital and enterprise | 生产要素 |
| rewards of factors | Rent (land), wages (labour), interest (capital), profit (enterprise) | 要素报酬 |
| specialisation | Concentrating on producing a limited range of goods/services | 专业化 |
| division of labour | Breaking production into separate tasks done by different workers | 分工 |
| production possibility curve (PPC) | Shows the maximum combinations of two goods an economy can produce with given resources; points inside = unused resources, outside = unattainable | 生产可能性曲线 |
| economic growth (PPC) | An outward shift of the PPC caused by more/better resources or technology | 经济增长（曲线外移） |
| choice | Deciding between alternatives because resources are scarce | 选择 |

## 计算与图示
- 机会成本题：答「被放弃的次优选择」本身，不写数字运算
- PPC 图：会画、会标 3 种点（线上=有效率、线内=资源闲置、线外=不可达）、会画外移（增长）与内移（灾害/资源流失）
- 常考辨析：movement along PPC（重新配置）vs shift of PPC（增长）
