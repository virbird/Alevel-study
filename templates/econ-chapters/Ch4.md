# Ch4 Government and the macroeconomy

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 4 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 4。

## 大纲目标
- 政府的宏观经济目标及其可能的冲突
- 财政政策、货币政策、供给侧政策的工具与效果
- 税收类型（直接/间接/累进）与政府支出
- 通胀的测量、成因与影响；失业的类型与成因

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| macroeconomic objectives | Economic growth, low unemployment, price stability, stable balance of payments, income redistribution, environmental protection | 宏观目标 |
| fiscal policy | Use of government spending and taxation to influence the economy | 财政政策 |
| monetary policy | Use of interest rates and money supply by the central bank | 货币政策 |
| supply-side policy | Policies to increase the productive potential of the economy (education, training, infrastructure) | 供给侧政策 |
| direct tax | Tax on income and profits (e.g. income tax) | 直接税 |
| indirect tax | Tax on goods and services (e.g. sales tax) | 间接税 |
| progressive tax | Tax where the rate rises as income rises | 累进税 |
| government expenditure | Spending by government on goods, services and transfer payments | 政府支出 |
| inflation | A sustained increase in the general price level | 通货膨胀 |
| demand-pull inflation | Inflation caused by rising aggregate demand | 需求拉动型通胀 |
| cost-push inflation | Inflation caused by rising costs of production | 成本推动型通胀 |
| consumer price index (CPI) | A measure of the average price of a basket of consumer goods and services | 消费者价格指数 |
| unemployment | People able and willing to work but without a job | 失业 |
| frictional unemployment | Short-term unemployment between jobs | 摩擦性失业 |
| structural unemployment | Unemployment due to a mismatch of skills/location or declining industries | 结构性失业 |
| seasonal unemployment | Unemployment due to seasonal patterns of work | 季节性失业 |
| cyclical (demand-deficient) unemployment | Unemployment caused by a fall in aggregate demand in a recession | 周期性失业 |
| economic growth | An increase in real GDP over time | 经济增长 |
| standard of living | The level of wealth, comfort and material goods available | 生活水平 |

## 计算与图示
- **通胀率 =（CPI 变化量 ÷ 基期 CPI）× 100**
- 税额计算：收入 × 税率（累进税注意分段）
- 政策方向题固定框架：目标 → 政策工具 → 传导链（如：提高利率 → 借贷成本上升 → 消费投资下降 → 需求下降 → 通胀缓解）
- 目标冲突经典例子：增长 vs 环境、低失业 vs 通胀
