# Ch5 Economic development

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 5 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 5。

## 大纲目标
- 区分经济增长与经济发展、发达国家与发展中国家
- HDI 的三个构成维度及其局限
- 生活水平差异的原因；贫困（绝对/相对）与不平等
- 人口结构指标；外援与国际债务的作用

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| economic development | Improvement in living standards and quality of life (broader than growth) | 经济发展 |
| developed country | A country with high income, industrialisation and living standards | 发达国家 |
| developing country | A country with lower income and living standards, still industrialising | 发展中国家 |
| human development index (HDI) | Composite measure: life expectancy, education and income per head | 人类发展指数 |
| GDP per head | GDP ÷ population; a rough measure of average income | 人均 GDP |
| absolute poverty | Inability to afford basic necessities for survival | 绝对贫困 |
| relative poverty | Income significantly below the average in a society | 相对贫困 |
| inequality | Unequal distribution of income/wealth across a population | 不平等 |
| birth rate | Number of live births per 1000 people per year | 出生率 |
| death rate | Number of deaths per 1000 people per year | 死亡率 |
| natural increase | Birth rate − death rate | 自然增长率 |
| population structure | The age and gender composition of a population | 人口结构 |
| foreign aid | Financial or technical assistance from one country to another | 外援 |
| international debt | Money owed by a country's government to foreign lenders | 国际债务 |
| quality of life | Broader wellbeing including health, environment and freedom | 生活质量 |

## 计算与图示
- **自然增长率 = 出生率 − 死亡率**（单位 ‰）
- HDI 对比题：会读 0–1 的指数并比较三个维度
- 常考辨析：growth 是量的增加（GDP），development 是质的改善（健康/教育/自由）
- 人口金字塔：会判读年轻型/老龄化结构的含义与影响
