# Ch3 Microeconomic decision makers

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 3 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 3。

## 大纲目标
- 家庭：收入来源、支出/储蓄/借贷的决定因素
- 工人：工资差异的原因、劳动力流动性、工会
- 银行：中央银行与商业银行的职能、利率的作用
- 企业：成本与收益的计算、规模经济/不经济、企业成长与存活原因

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| income | Money received from wages, rent, interest, profit or transfers | 收入 |
| expenditure | Spending on goods and services | 支出 |
| saving | Income not spent on current consumption | 储蓄 |
| borrowing | Obtaining money now to be repaid later with interest | 借贷 |
| wage | Payment to labour, usually per hour or per period | 工资 |
| labour mobility | The ease with which workers can move between jobs/areas | 劳动力流动性 |
| trade union | An organisation of workers that bargains for better pay and conditions | 工会 |
| commercial bank | A financial institution that accepts deposits and makes loans to households and firms | 商业银行 |
| central bank | The government's bank; controls interest rates and money supply | 中央银行 |
| interest rate | The cost of borrowing / reward for saving, expressed as a percentage | 利率 |
| fixed costs | Costs that do not vary with output in the short run (e.g. rent) | 固定成本 |
| variable costs | Costs that vary with output (e.g. raw materials) | 可变成本 |
| total costs | Fixed costs + variable costs | 总成本 |
| average cost | Total cost ÷ output (cost per unit) | 平均成本 |
| total revenue | Price × quantity sold | 总收入 |
| profit | Total revenue − total costs | 利润 |
| productivity | Output per unit of input (e.g. per worker per hour) | 生产率 |
| economies of scale | Falling average costs as a firm grows (purchasing, technical, financial, managerial, marketing, risk-bearing) | 规模经济 |
| diseconomies of scale | Rising average costs as a firm becomes too large (communication, coordination, motivation) | 规模不经济 |
| business growth | Expansion of a firm by internal growth or mergers/takeovers | 企业成长 |

## 计算与图示
- **TC = FC + VC**；**AC = TC ÷ Q**
- **TR = P × Q**；**Profit = TR − TC**
- 生产率 = 产出 ÷ 投入（会解释提高生产率的意义）
- 工资差异分析框架：技能/教育、工作风险、劳动力供需、工会力量、歧视
- 常见题型：给表格算成本/利润并解释企业决策
