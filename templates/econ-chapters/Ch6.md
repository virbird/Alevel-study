# Ch6 International trade and globalisation

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 6 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 6。

## 大纲目标
- 国际贸易的原因（国家间专业化）与贸易利得
- 汇率变动的含义与影响（升值/贬值对进出口、经常账户的作用）
- 经常账户的构成；顺差与逆差
- 贸易保护的三种手段；跨国公司与全球化的动因与影响

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| international trade | Exchange of goods and services between countries | 国际贸易 |
| specialisation (countries) | Countries concentrating on producing goods where they have an advantage | 国家专业化 |
| exchange rate | The price of one currency in terms of another | 汇率 |
| appreciation | An increase in the value of a currency (imports cheaper, exports dearer) | 货币升值 |
| depreciation | A fall in the value of a currency (imports dearer, exports cheaper) | 货币贬值 |
| current account | Record of trade in goods and services, income and current transfers | 经常账户 |
| trade surplus | Value of exports > value of imports | 贸易顺差 |
| trade deficit | Value of imports > value of exports | 贸易逆差 |
| trade protection | Measures to shield domestic producers from foreign competition | 贸易保护 |
| tariff | A tax on imports | 关税 |
| quota | A physical limit on the quantity of imports | 配额 |
| export subsidy | Payment to domestic producers to make exports cheaper | 出口补贴 |
| multinational corporation (MNC) | A firm operating in more than one country | 跨国公司 |
| globalisation | Increasing integration of economies through trade, capital and labour flows | 全球化 |
| balance of payments | Record of all financial transactions between a country and the rest of the world | 国际收支 |

## 计算与图示
- 汇率换算：外币金额 × 汇率（注意标价方向）
- 汇率影响分析框架：升值 → 进口便宜/出口贵 → 经常账户恶化（数量反应看弹性）
- 经常账户 = 货物贸易 + 服务贸易 + 收入 + 经常转移（会分类记账目）
- 全球化利弊各列 3 条以上：利（选择/价格/就业/技术），弊（依赖/文化/环境/不平等）
