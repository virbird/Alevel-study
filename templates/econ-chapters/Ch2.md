# Ch2 The allocation of resources

> 来源：CIE IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus（2025-2027）Topic 2 整理；定义为 syllabus 口径，**教材原文以课本为准**。
> 对应教材：Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Economics Coursebook（2025 新版）Unit 2。

## 大纲目标
- 用供需模型解释价格决定与变动（均衡价格、超额需求/供给）
- 理解价格机制的功能；比较市场经济、混合经济、计划经济
- 掌握 PED/PES 的定义、计算、影响因素与厂商/政府应用
- 理解市场失灵、外部性、优值品/劣值品及政府干预手段

## 术语
| Term (EN) | 定义 (EN) | 中文提示 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| demand | The quantity of a good consumers are willing and able to buy at each price | 需求 |
| supply | The quantity of a good producers are willing and able to sell at each price | 供给 |
| market equilibrium | Where demand equals supply; the market-clearing price | 市场均衡 |
| price mechanism | The system where price changes allocate resources (rations, signals, incentives) | 价格机制 |
| price elasticity of demand (PED) | Responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price | 需求价格弹性 |
| price elasticity of supply (PES) | Responsiveness of quantity supplied to a change in price | 供给价格弹性 |
| market economy | Resources allocated by private firms through the price mechanism | 市场经济 |
| mixed economy | Resources allocated by both private sector and government | 混合经济 |
| planned economy | Resources allocated by government decisions | 计划经济 |
| market failure | When the price mechanism leads to a misallocation of resources | 市场失灵 |
| externalities | Costs or benefits affecting third parties not involved in the transaction | 外部性 |
| merit goods | Goods with positive externalities, under-consumed if left to the market (e.g. education) | 优值品 |
| demerit goods | Goods with negative externalities, over-consumed if left to the market (e.g. tobacco) | 劣值品 |
| subsidy | A payment by government to producers to lower costs and increase supply | 补贴 |
| indirect tax | A tax on goods and services, raising price and reducing demand | 间接税 |
| maximum price | A legal upper limit on price set by government | 最高限价 |
| minimum price | A legal lower limit on price set by government | 最低限价 |

## 计算与图示
- **PED = %ΔQd ÷ %ΔP**；**PES = %ΔQs ÷ %ΔP**
- 百分比变化 =（变化量 ÷ 原值）× 100
- 判读：|PED| > 1 弹性（elastic），< 1 缺乏弹性（inelastic）
- 应用：inelastic 商品涨价可增加厂商总收入；政府对 demerit goods 征税、对 merit goods 补贴的方向分析
- 图：供需曲线、均衡移动（需求/供给变动因素要会列举：收入、替代品价格、成本、技术等）
